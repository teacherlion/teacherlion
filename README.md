### 🧠 What I'm working on

* 🌐 **Web applications with Nuxt** — corporate portals, internal systems and member areas,
  built to load fast and stay easy to maintain.
* 🖥️ **Self-hosted infrastructure** — I run my own VPS with Docker, Caddy and CI/CD, and I
  monitor uptime on the services I keep online.
* 🔐 **Security, as a study** — I apply what I learn to what I build: security headers, strict
  CSP, input handling and care with secrets. It's the direction I want my career to take, and
  I'm learning in the open rather than claiming expertise.
